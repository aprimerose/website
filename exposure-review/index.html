Exposure Review

What a stranger can find about you in public sources, ranked by what to close first.

One person, one review

A fake recruiter, investor or partner starts from what is public: your role, your company, your profiles, the ways to reach you. The more of it a message gets right, the more it reads like it comes from someone who knows you.

We list what is public about you, from the pages you open for us. Each item gets one of four defences: remove it, lock it down, separate it, or watch it. Thirty days later we capture the same pages again and show what is closed.

On your own signed request, and about you only. CISSP-led.
